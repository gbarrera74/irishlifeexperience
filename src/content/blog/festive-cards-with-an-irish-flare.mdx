This year be sure to send holiday wishes with adorable cards from a very creative artist in Ireland. They are the perfect way to wish your loved ones a Merry Christmas and Happy New Year.

Susan McGing studied in the creative stream of the DIT Postgrad Diploma in Advertising. She has worked as a content creator and copywriter for clients such as EPIC, Fáilte Ireland, Tourism Ireland, and Trinity College.

Susan’s biggest passion lies with storytelling, as she thrives from brainstorming with a team and telling a story through exciting work.

She currently works as a freelance illustrator, running her own illustration shop (Susan Illustrates) where she sells cards, commissions, and prints of her own designs.

You can check out her [Etsy Shop](https://www.etsy.com/shop/susanillustrates/) where you can purchase her cards. They will definitely be a nice touch of Irish flare this Holiday Season.
