Hi Everyone!

I’m Liam, and I will be one of your Junior Counselors helping you adjust to Ireland! I will be 17 at the time that we leave for Ireland, and I am super excited to meet you all!

I am from southwest Montana, and have been doing the Irish Life Experience Program for two years, making this my third. I am super excited to see everyone going to a new (Or familiar) place!

I am currently a Ski Patroller in training and am a certified Wilderness First Responder. I like to ski, go driving, and play games with my friends. A fun fact about me is that although I am from Montana, I go to school on the East Coast.

And a personal tip from me is to make sure to bring a card game so that you can find common interests with some of the other travelers!

See you soon!
