Hi Everyone!

I am Tyler, and I am excited for my fourth trip with the Irish Life Experience! I actually went on this program as a student, so I know how impactful and memorable it can be. Coming back as a Counselor for the third time is something I am really grateful for.

I am from Montana and studied finance at Carroll College. I have had the chance to travel and work in a variety of places, including studying abroad in Australia. My background includes tech, travel, and financial services, where I now help people plan for their future.

Outside of work, I enjoy hiking, traveling, photography, pottery, and officiating basketball.

I am here to help make this an unforgettable experience-can not wait to meet you all and explore the Emerald Isle together.
