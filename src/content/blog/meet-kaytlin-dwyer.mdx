Hello!

My name is Kaytlin Dwyer, and I am from Springdale, Arkansas. I am a graduating senior at the University of Arkansas, where I majored in Anthropology, a field that has deepened my appreciation for culture, history, and human connection.

I am also an Open Champion Irish dancer and have been competing and performing for 17 years. Irish dance has been such a meaningful part of my life, shaping not only my discipline and work ethic but also my love for Irish culture and traditions.

This will be my first time traveling to Ireland, which makes this experience even more special. I am incredibly excited to finally visit a place that has been such a big part of my life through dance. I feel so grateful to share this journey with such an amazing group, and I can not wait to meet everyone, teach some Irish dance, and experience all that Ireland has to offer together.

I am especially looking forward to making lasting memories, building connections, and celebrating Irish culture in a whole new way!

See you soon!
