Are you searching for the perfect gift for that special college or high school student? Search no longer! Give them the gift of a lifetime this Holiday Season. A gift they will truly remember and treasure forever.

The Irish Life Experience offers two exceptional summer study abroad programs-one for American and Canadian college students and a separate program for high school students. Emphasizing culture, education, travel, and fun, we strive to ensure each student’s experience is unique and unforgettable.

What joy you will bring to them on Christmas morning, as they unwrap this magical gift of Ireland. Please help make their dreams come true by contributing to their Irish Life Experience.
