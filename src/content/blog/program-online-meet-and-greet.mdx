With the 2023 Irish Life Experience just around the corner, we would like to invite parents and students to a very special online “Meet and Greet.”

**What**: Join the Irish Life Experience Team and meet your ILE Staff and fellow participants from around the country.

**When**: **June 12th at 6:00 PM Eastern Standard Time.**

**Where**: Zoom (An invitation will be sent out to parents’ and participants’ e-mails prior.)

**Why**: This will be a wonderful way to meet some of the students who will be attending the program and a good time to have any of your questions answered before the program departs.

**Schedule**: Brief Introductions (Name, age, where you live, hobbies, and why you are looking forward to traveling to Ireland.) followed by Q & A’s.

Pretty soon we will be zooming all around Ireland, so what better way to get the trip started, then by zooming at home.
