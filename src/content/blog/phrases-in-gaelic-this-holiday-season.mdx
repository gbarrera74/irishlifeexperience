Would you like to impress your loved ones this Holiday Season and learn a few phrases in Gaelic? Now is your chance!

**Christmas happiness be with you! ** Nollaig shona duit! _\<\<Noll-ag hunna ditch>> _

**A happy and prosperous Christmas to you! **Nollaig faoi shéan is faoi shaonas duit! \<\<_Noll-ag fwee shee-an iss fwee show-nas-ditch_>>

**The blessings of Christmas be with you!** Beannachtaí na Nollag duit! _\<\<Ban-ach-tee na noll-ag ditch>>_

**Happy New Year! **Athbhliain faoi mhaise duit! _\<\<Ah-vleen fwee vosh-ah ditch>>_

**Season’s Greetings! **Beannachtaí an tSéasúir! _\<\<Ban-ach-tee on tay-sur>>_
