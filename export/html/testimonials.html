Have you ever looked up at the sky, watching as it changes from robin’s-egg blue to slate grey, gorgeously streaked with reds and oranges and pinks? Staring and wondering in awe as thousands of stars begin to pinprick the inky darkness, as if the world itself was scattered with millions of fiery dreams? That grand, humbling, deep-set sensation of realizing how majestically massive the world really is, and how tiny you are in relation to it, is truly the only way I can properly describe Ireland. No words-no poetry, songs, music, books-or even a collection of books-can truly describe the Irish Life Experience and the magic that ebbs from deep within The Emerald Isle itself. I can truly only relate it to nature’s most powerful masterpieces-the churning roar of the infinite ocean, swelling and sparkling with mystery and power. The remarkable might of a minute seed, growing and spreading roots deep into the earth, poking its tiny head from the loamy soil, stretching twiggy branches to the sky and, ever so slowly, becoming an ancient, steady, gnarled tree. I remember, in my daze of exhaustion and excitement and homesickness and all other sorts of emotions, staring out of one of the Aer Lingus plane windows and catching my first glimpse of Ireland: A tiny, quaint lighthouse, resting on an even tinier, quainter island. My mind instantly exploded with bursts of realization-I was actually in Ireland! I was so excited, I felt as if I might spontaneously combust, although I figured travel insurance-or the flight attendants-might not be all that terribly pleased with me. Getting off the plane, feeling that first gentle breeze, catching my first views of the rolling green hills and little fields of grazing, cloud-like sheep, it all meant the world to me. I do not think I ever stopped smiling or gaping in awe at everything around me! Seriously! I would get excited over the most mundane of things, even the street signs-with their Irish and English descriptions-excited me! I started every morning with a smile, anticipatedly enthralled for what the day would bring, and ended the evening with a smile, wildly impressed, once more, by Ireland’s magical grandeur. Not a single day-not even an hour-goes past without me remembering some ridiculous or glorious memory of the ILE and the family I found there. I will be doing dishes and zoning off, recalling all of Ireland’s wonderful evenings spent journaling with my dear friend, Taryn having hilarious dance-offs in the bus isleways, conversing with Michael in Gaelic, or the fascinating, humorously intriguing classes. The connections I made will last with me forever-I became closer to these people in three weeks than I had with most of my friends in eleven years of school! With all of my deepest gratitude-thank you to Taryn, with her deep-set love and compassion and openness. Thank you to Kaylee and Clare and Kylene and Miles-you all made Ireland feel even more like a home. And thank you to all of my dear friends-without you, Ireland would not be half as magical and wondrous! And to all of you reading this-trust me. Going on the Irish Life Experience was the greatest decision of my life, and I truly hope you will all choose to make that same, miraculous decision as well!
